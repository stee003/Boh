# Gameplay

Fights are short and readable. Movement helps you take space. It is not required to land a shot, and it does not make people unhittable.

Personal score examples: elimination +100, assist +50, capture +150, objective defense +75, objective elimination +125, support +50. Respawn targets 2–5 seconds. Private matches can change time, score, health, respawn, friendly fire, and regeneration.

## Weapons and operators

Twenty-five weapons across assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, snipers, marksman rifles, LMGs, pistols, experimental arms, and melee. Recoil patterns are fixed. Attachments trade range, reload, recoil, or mobility. They do not raise damage. Weapon level unlocks those sidegrades; it does not make the gun stronger.

Twelve operators. Tactical and ultimate abilities open a fight or hold a lane. They do not end a full-health duel by themselves.

## Drills

Empty seats fill with drills. Difficulty changes reaction, pathing, and target choice. It does not add aim bonus. Drills do not move official rating.

## Ranked

Winning matters more than the kill column. Repeated ranked abandons lock the queue for ten minutes. A dropped relay is a fault and is not punished. Fewer than two humans is not an official rating match.
